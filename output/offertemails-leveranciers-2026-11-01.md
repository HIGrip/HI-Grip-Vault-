# Offertemails leveranciers (concept, versturen doet Timo)

13 geel gemarkeerde fabrikanten uit `2026-11-01-leveranciers-per-regio.xlsx`. Eén basismail, per fabrikant alleen een eigen openingszin en het adres. Stuur elke mail los (geen CC/BCC naar meerdere fabrieken).

De mail noemt geen huidige producten: hij geldt voor de eigen collectie én voor clubs met eigen wensen. **SKU** = één ontwerp in één kleur en één maat. De prijs wordt gevraagd voor een standaard crew-gripsok, plus wat opties (lengte, garen, logo) aan de prijs doen.

---

## Basismail

**Onderwerp:** Quote request private label grip socks, MOQ 50 to 2,000 pairs per SKU

Hi [naam of team],

[EIGEN OPENINGSZIN]

We are HÏ Grip, a Dutch brand of performance grip socks. Besides our own collection, we produce custom grip socks for sports clubs and teams. We supply the designs ourselves (tech pack, artwork and colours), so we are looking for a manufacturer for production only.

Every order can be different: sock length, colours, logo and sizes depend on the brand or club. What they have in common is silicone grip on the sole and a sporty, technical quality.

By SKU we mean one design in one colour and one size.

**Could you give an indicative price per pair for a standard crew grip sock at these quantities per SKU?**

**And please let us know:**
1. Your real minimum per SKU, and whether the minimum applies per SKU, per colourway or per design
2. One-time costs per design (setup, programming, silicone print screens or moulds)
3. Whether the price is EXW or delivered to the Netherlands (DDP), and your payment terms
4. Packaging options (polybag, header card, paper wrap) and their cost
5. Your quality control process and certificates (for example OEKO-TEX, ISO, BSCI)
6. Whether you already produce socks with silicone grip pads, and for which type of brand
7. Which options you offer (lengths, yarns, compression, cushioning, knitted logos) and how they affect the price
8. Sample cost and timing: how fast you can send a proof photo and a physical sample

We aim for delivery within 6 weeks after sample approval. If you have a price list or catalogue of grip or sports socks, we would be happy to receive it as well.
